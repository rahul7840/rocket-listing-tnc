Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

How the Rocket Listing Chrome extension handles your data, and the terms for using it.

Effective date: September 8, 2026 Last updated: September 8, 2026 Product: Rocket Listing (Chrome Extension)
In short: Rocket Listing runs entirely inside your browser. Your recordings, listing templates and settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage APIs. We do not operate a backend server, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your browsing data, product data, or recorded actions with anyone.

1 Who we are

Rocket Listing is a Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that helps sellers automate product listing workflows on marketplaces such as Meesho, Flipkart and Amazon — by recording DOM-based actions once and replaying them later to fill forms and complete repetitive seller tasks.

2 Data we access and why

To do its job, the extension requests the following browser permissions. Here is exactly what each one is used for:

PermissionPurpose
activeTab / tabsKnow which tab you're working in so recording and replay target the right page.
scriptingInject the recorder/replayer into the page you're actively using.
storage / unlimitedStorageSave your recordings, listing templates and preferences locally in your browser (IndexedDB / chrome.storage).
webNavigationDetect page navigations so a recorded workflow keeps working across multi-page or SPA flows.
downloadsLet you export a recording or listing data to a file on your device, on your request.
sidePanelShow the extension's control panel alongside the page you're working on.
host_permissions (all sites)Allow recording/replay on whichever marketplace or site you choose to use it on — the extension does not act unless you start it.

3 Where your data lives

Everything Rocket Listing captures — recorded actions, element selectors, listing field values, and your settings — is written to your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and chrome.storage) on your own device.

✓ Stored locally, on-device ✓ Not uploaded to any server ✓ Not shared with third parties ✓ Not sold, ever

Uninstalling the extension, or clearing your browser's extension data, permanently deletes this locally stored information. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it for you.

4 Third-party requests

The extension loads a small number of static UI icons from a third-party CDN (icons8.com) for its popup interface. No personal data, page content, or recorded actions are sent as part of these requests — only a standard image request occurs, subject to that provider's own handling of network requests (e.g. IP address in server logs), which is outside our control.

Rocket Listing does not call any AI/LLM API, analytics service, or advertising network, and does not embed any tracking pixels or third-party analytics SDKs.

5 What we do not do

  • No account creation or login required
  • No collection of personal information (name, email, payment details)
  • No sale of user data to third parties
  • No behavioral advertising or ad targeting

6 Your choices & control

You can review, export, or delete any recording from within the extension's side panel at any time. You can revoke site access for specific domains or remove the extension entirely from chrome://extensions, which removes all locally stored data associated with it.

7 Children's privacy

Rocket Listing is a productivity tool intended for business sellers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future version introduces optional cloud sync — we will update the "Last updated" date above and describe the change here before it takes effect.
