Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Rocket Listing collects only the information it needs to work: to sign you in, to save and sync your listing templates, to show your Meesho payment and profit figures, and to generate listing text for you. We use it only for those purposes — to make your selling simpler — and nothing else.

Effective October 7, 2026Last updated October 7, 2026Rocket Listing (Chrome extension)

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Rocket Listing is a Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that helps online sellers on marketplaces such as Meesho, Flipkart and Amazon save time: it records a product-listing workflow once and replays it to fill forms, helps generate listing text, and shows finance and profitability figures for your seller account.

This policy explains what information the extension collects, how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Rocket Listing extension and the Rocket Listing service that it connects to (“the Service”). “We”, “us” and “our” mean the Rocket Listing team.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is listed below. The last column shows whether the information leaves your device.

InformationWhat it includesSent to our servers?
Google account details When you choose “Sign in with Google”: your Google user ID, email address, display name and profile photo URL. We never see your Google password. Yes — to create and recognise your account.
Listing templates & recordings The workflow you record: the site and page address, page title, the steps you take (clicks, text you type into listing fields, field labels and selectors), and basic technical details — browser name, extension version, user-agent string and window size. We do not collect your browsing history, and we do not track which sites you visit.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to provide and improve the features you use:

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to save, sync, list and replay your listing templates;
  • to fetch and display your finance, earnings and profit figures;
  • to generate the listing text you request;
  • to apply plan and usage limits (for example, how many templates you can save, how many listings you can fill with a template, and how often payments can be synced);
  • to fix bugs and keep the Service working, and to respond when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. We do not use it for any purpose unrelated to Rocket Listing.

4. We do not sell your data

  • Never sold
  • Never shared for advertising
  • Used only for your purpose

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, now or in the future. The information described in this policy is collected solely so that Rocket Listing can do what you installed it to do, and it is used only for that purpose.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Google — to sign you in, using Google’s own sign-in. Google handles your sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that work for us — such as the hosting and database providers that run our servers, and the AI model provider that processes listing-text requests. They process information only on our instructions, only to provide the Service, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal reasons — if the law, a court order or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the Service.
  • A change of ownership — if Rocket Listing is merged with or acquired by another company, your information may transfer to it, and this policy would continue to apply until you are told of any change.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers or any other third party.

Third-party requests from the extension’s interface. The side panel loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and a few icons from icons8.com. These are ordinary web requests; no recorded actions, listing data or account details are sent with them, but those providers may see your IP address, as with any website. The Meesho category lookup sends only numeric category IDs. The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

6. Storage, security and retention

Where it is stored. Information kept on your device is held in your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB and chrome.storage). Information that is synced is stored on our servers in the databases that run the Service. Information sent between the extension and our servers travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and access to it is limited to the people and systems that need it to run the Service.

Sign-in. After you sign in, the extension keeps a Rocket Listing session token and your basic profile (name, email, photo) in your browser. Your Google access token stays in Chrome’s own identity cache. Signing out removes the session token and profile from the extension and revokes the Google token.

How long we keep it. We keep your account information and the templates and finance data you have synced for as long as your account is active, so that the Service keeps working for you. When you ask us to delete your account or data, we delete it within 30 days, except where we are required by law to keep something. Information stored only on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the extension.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Review and delete templates from the extension’s side panel at any time.
  • Sign out from the side panel. This revokes the Google token and clears your session from the extension.
  • Stop sharing finance data by simply not pressing the payments sync button — it only runs when you start it and confirm.
  • Access, correct or delete your data. To receive a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have your account and all synced data deleted, email us at the address in section 12 from the email address on your account. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Remove the extension from chrome://extensions. This deletes everything stored on your device. It does not by itself delete data already synced to our servers, so email us if you also want that deleted.
  • Revoke Google access at any time from your Google Account’s security settings.

9. Google user data (Limited Use)

Rocket Listing’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request only the openid, email and profile scopes, and we use that information only to sign you in and identify your account. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it except as needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your consent.

10. Children’s privacy

Rocket Listing is a business tool for sellers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change takes effect, and for material changes we will also tell you in the extension or by email. Continuing to use Rocket Listing after a change means you accept the updated policy.
